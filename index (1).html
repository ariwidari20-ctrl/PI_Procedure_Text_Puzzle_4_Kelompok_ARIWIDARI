MATERI INTI

What is a Procedure Text?

A procedure text explains how to make something or how to do something through clear, ordered instructions.

Social Function

To explain how to make or do something.

Generic Structure

Goal → Ingredients / Materials → Steps

Language Features

Imperative verbs, action verbs, and sequence connectors.

Model Text: Making a Cup of Tea

Goal
To make a cup of tea.
Ingredients / Materials
A tea bag, hot water, sugar (optional), a cup, and a spoon.
Steps
1. First, put a tea bag into a cup.
2. Next, pour hot water into the cup.
3. Then, let the tea brew for a few minutes.
4. After that, add sugar and stir well if you like.
5. Finally, remove the tea bag and serve the tea.

Imperative verbs: put, pour, let, add, stir, remove, serveSequence connectors: first, next, then, after that, finally

Keselamatan: air panas hanya ditangani guru/orang dewasa. Gunakan simulasi aman jika diperlukan.
